> Over the decades, Farkas' style has become simpler and his musical texture denser. This can be seen in the song cycle "Virágoskert" (Garden of Flowers) which he composed at the age of 85. In the seven melodies that make up this cycle, Farkas drew inspiration from texts by different Hungarian poets, each depicting a particular flower :
>
> Margaréta (daisy) - Fehér virág (white flower) - Leánykökörcsin (anemone) - Klematisz (clematis) - Napraforgó (sunflower) - Krizantém (chrysanthemum) - Derengö rózsa (morning rose).
> {{< author name="László Gombos" >}}

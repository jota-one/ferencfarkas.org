> Ferenc Farkas wrote "Régi temető" (Old Cemetery) at the age of 90. In the seven melodies that make up the work, the composer took texts from a cycle of epitaphs by the Hungarian poet Sándor Weöres, tinged with black humor, irony or rustic philosophy, where fictional dead people speak from their tombs to tell about their life or their death.
> {{< author name="László Gombos" >}}

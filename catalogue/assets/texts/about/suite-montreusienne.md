> The "Suite montreusienne" was composed in 1988 at the request of the Nouvel Orchestre de Montreux and its conductor, my son András Farkas.  
>
> The first movement, "En route," (On the way) is a dynamic opening, followed by "Promenade d'automne" (Autumn walk), which evokes the falling of leaves in autumn. In the middle can be heard a few bars of the famous "Le vigneron monte à sa vigne" (The vinedresser climbs to his vineyard), a song inseparable from Montreux. Next comes a "Danse en costumes anciens" (Dance in early costumes), a minuet in the style of traditional Montreux folk dances. The fourth movement, "Souvenirs de jeunesse" (Memories of Youth), reflects my impressions from a stay I spent in the Montreux region in 1929. 
> {{< author name="Ferenc Farkas" >}}

> The “Dialoghi” for two instruments were composed in 1987. This three-movement work actually forms a single, expansive arc within which all manner of dialogue between the two instruments can unfold.
>
> In the “Moderato cantabile,” the voices are interwoven with great finesse. The second movement, “Scherzo,” resembles a graceful and playful pursuit, unfolding a rich palette of ideas. Amidst the complexity of the rhythmic or melodic counterpoint, the two instruments rarely come together; it is only in the final four measures that they move in parallel. The third movement, “Allegro leggero,” built upon complementary motions, leads into a triumphant finale where the work’s pantomimic gestures converge in a display full of vivacity.
> {{< author name="László Gombos" >}}

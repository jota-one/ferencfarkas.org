> The 20th century artist likes to hide behind forgotten masks. Thus the verses of Rainer Maria Rilke: « O sage, Dichter, was du tust? Ich rühme. Woher dein Recht in jeglichem Kostüme, in jeder Maske wahr zu sein? Ich rühme » (O tell us, poet, what do you do? I praise. Whence comes your right, in every costume,
in every mask, to be true? I praise).
>
> In the "Three Dance Paraphrases," I adapted old dances from the 14th and 16th centuries, exploring ways to interweave these melodies with twelve-tone countersubjects and secondary parts.
>
>The first movement "Allegretto" is a varied dance form, the second movement "Moderato" has a three-part form, the third, "Allegro", is a rondo. I have put together a set of instruments including a string instrument (viola), a wind instrument (flute) and a brass instrument (horn).
> {{< author name="Ferenc Farkas" >}}

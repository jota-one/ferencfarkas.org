> I composed these two short pieces at different times. Later, they were brought together and published under the title "Egloga – Burattinata" by Editio Musica Budapest.
>
> Tender and pastoral in character, "Egloga" is dedicated to Jean-Louis Matthey, a flutist and librarian in Lausanne (Switzerland). I met him on the occasion of the wedding of my son, András, to Françoise Viquerat, a young Swiss woman. It is to him that I owe the first catalogue of my works : « Inventaire du fonds musical Ferenc Farkas : Catalogue des œuvres », published by the Cantonal and University Library of Lausanne in 1979.
>
> "Burattinata" is a lively, playful, and humorous work, imitating the jerky, bouncy movements of puppets. I composed it in 1977, based on an earlier piece for violin and piano, and dedicated it to the artists who first performed it, Gottfried Hechtl and Leo Witoszynsky.
> {{< author name="Ferenc Farkas" >}}
